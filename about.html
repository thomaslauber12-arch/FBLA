About Our Chapter

Building character, confidence, and real-world business expertise.

What is FBLA?

Future Business Leaders of America (FBLA) is the largest career student business organization in the world. It helps high school students prepare for careers in business through academic competitions, leadership development, and community service programs.

Core Mission

Our mission at Southeast Bulloch High School is to inspire and prepare community-minded student leaders for success in a global economy through relevant career experiences and leadership development.

Why Join SEHS FBLA?

  • Competitive Edge: Over 70 state and national events in finance, coding, design, and public speaking.
  • Leadership Roles: Local officer opportunities and state leadership delegate roles.
  • Networking & Recognition: College resume building, scholarships, and state-wide conference trips.
