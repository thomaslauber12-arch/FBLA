Mission & Values

Building Tomorrow's Business Leaders

Southeast Bulloch High School FBLA brings business education and students together in a positive working relationship through innovative leadership and career development programs.

🎯

Our Mission

FBLA inspires and prepares students to become community-minded business leaders in a global society through relevant career preparation and leadership experiences.

🚀

Our Vision

To empower every Southeast Bulloch High School member with practical business knowledge, public speaking confidence, and ethical decision-making capabilities.

🏆

Excellence & Competition

Our chapter regularly competes at Regional, State, and National Leadership Conferences across financial analysis, software development, and public speaking.

Core Pillars of FBLA

Four strategic focuses guiding our chapter's calendar and student development.

Pillar 01

Leadership Development

Participate in workshops, officer roles, and committee positions that teach project management, delegation, and strategic planning.

Pillar 02

Competitive Events

Test your academic knowledge and technical prowess in over 70 official FBLA events spanning finance, IT, design, and marketing.

Pillar 03

Community Service

Engage with Bulloch County through food drives, local business partnerships, and charitable initiatives supporting March of Dimes.
